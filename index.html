Agent Passport

A free, non-transferable passport for Agent ID holders. Mint yours, check in once a day to build your streak, and collect soulbound badges at 3, 7, 14 and 21 days.

Minting is free — you only pay network gas. No value, return or reward is promised. Your wallet signs every transaction itself; this site never signs anything for you and never asks for a seed phrase or private key.
